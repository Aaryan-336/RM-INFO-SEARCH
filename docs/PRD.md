# RM Intelligence Platform

## Objective

Allow a Relationship Manager to enter:

* Person Name
* Company Name

and receive a complete intelligence report within 30-60 seconds.

---

## Primary Goal

Priority order:

1. Mobile Number
2. Email Address
3. Current Company Details
4. Professional Experience
5. Directorships
6. Social Presence
7. Company Intelligence
8. Net Worth Signals
9. AI Generated RM Brief

---

## Input

```json
{
  "name": "John Doe",
  "company": "Comapny Name"
}
```

---

## Output

### Contact Intelligence

* Business Mobile Number
* Business Email
* LinkedIn URL
* Contact Confidence Score
* Source Attribution

### Person Intelligence

* Current Designation
* Experience Summary
* Previous Companies
* Education
* Certifications
* Public Mentions

### Company Intelligence

* CIN
* Industry
* Revenue
* AUM
* Registered Address
* Directors
* Subsidiaries

### Corporate Intelligence

* Directorships
* Group Companies
* Related Entities

### AI RM Brief

* Background Summary
* Conversation Starters
* Industry Context
* Important Talking Points
